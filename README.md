# Sannah
### Connecting Dental Students with Patients for Better Oral Care

## 1. Project Idea
Sannah is a platform that connects dental students with patients seeking dental care. Students can showcase the dental services and cases they need for their clinical training, while patients can explore available services and book appointments.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The project's success will be evaluated through the following indicators:

- Ease of registration for both students and patients.
- Ability of patients to find suitable dental students and book appointments.
- Ability of students to showcase their services and clinical cases.
- Ease of navigation and overall user experience.
- User satisfaction based on usability testing and feedback.
